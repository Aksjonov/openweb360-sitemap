How we cook

Smashed to order

Every burger starts as a ball of fresh chuck and brisket. It hits the griddle, gets pressed flat, and sears in its own fat until the crust turns deep brown.

We flip once, add American or sharp cheddar, and stack it on a butter-toasted potato bun. No heat lamps, no pre-cooked patties.
